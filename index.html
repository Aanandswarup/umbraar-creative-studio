Studio

01 / 06

We are fourteen designers, engineers and artists in Shoreditch. No account managers, no template factory — the people who pitch the work also ship it.

Clients: cultural institutions, independent labels, and the occasional Formula 1 team that wants something nobody has seen before.

Every site we ship gets judged twice: once by the people who visit it, and once by the machines that deliver it. We build for both. A point of view in the art direction, a director behind the motion, and code that holds sixty frames a second on a five-year-old phone.

Capabilities

03 / 06 — Six disciplines

Art direction
01

Art direction

ConceptIdentityEditorial

One idea, carried all the way through. We decide what the work is about before deciding how it moves — grids, type scales, palette and the moments where the grid is allowed to break.

02

Creative development

Front-endCanvasPerformance

Hand-written front-end that survives contact with real devices. No framework tax where it is not earned, no 4MB bundle for a landing page. This site is the demo: zero runtime dependencies.

03

WebGL & shaders

GLSLThree.jsRay marching

Real-time graphics written to run everywhere, including mid-range Android. Complexity is budgeted per frame: we would rather ship one perfect effect at 60fps than five at nineteen.

04

Motion systems

ChoreographyScrollTransitions

Motion with a director, not a library. Every transition answers a question — where did I come from, where am I going, what changed — and there is always a reduced-motion path through it.

05

Interaction design

PrototypingAccessibilityWCAG 2.2

Interfaces prototyped in the browser, not in a deck. Keyboard paths, focus order and screen-reader labels are part of the design review, not a cleanup ticket.

06

Design systems

TokensDocsHandover

Tokens, components and documentation your team can actually extend after we leave. The handover is a working repository, not a zip of screenshots.

Process

04 / 06 — Ten weeks, typically

Four movements. One continuous conversation.

Fixed fee, fixed dates, no surprise change orders. You see the work in the browser from week two onwards.

01

Discovery & direction

Two weeks of interrogation. We read everything you have, talk to the people who will use it, and come back with three directions that are genuinely different — not three shades of the same idea.

02

Art direction

We choose one direction and push it hard: type, grid, palette, motion language, and the first real screens. Everything you see is built at the true size in the browser.

03

Engineering

The build is the design review. Shaders, scroll choreography and transitions are tuned frame by frame on real hardware, with a performance budget we refuse to negotiate.

04

Launch & care

We ship, watch the graphs, and stay for the first month. After that you get the repository, the documentation, and a standing invitation to call.
